Rock Paper Scissors AI Challenge

Challenge our AI in a classic game of Rock, Paper, Scissors. This isn't just a random number game; it uses Computer Vision via your webcam to recognize your hand gestures in real-time! Powered by TensorFlow.js and Google's Teachable Machine, this demo showcases how AI can run directly in your browser.

Instructions

  • Click "Start Game" to enable your webcam.
  • Show your hand (Rock, Paper, or Scissors) to the camera.
  • Click "Shoot!" to lock in your move.
  • See if you can beat the computer!
Press "Start Game" to begin!
